# Daily Check Prompt

```text
Use $running-coach to analyze my last 7 days of watch (COROS or Garmin) sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, recovery, training load, and recent runs. Tell me whether I should run today, what session to do, stop rules, and a safer downshift option.
```
