# Marathon Plan Prompt

```text
Use $running-coach to evaluate my marathon goal. Use watch fitness (COROS or Garmin), race prediction, recent 60-90 day training history, long runs, load ratio, readiness, weather risk, and fueling needs. Give conservative, main, and aggressive targets plus the next 4 weeks of training.
```
